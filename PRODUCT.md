# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone who might hire a filmmaker in or around Dar es Salaam: NGOs and organizations (schools, charities, social impact programs), people with events (launches, celebrations), brands and businesses (ads, company films, reels), and musicians and artists. Most visitors arrive on a phone, often from an Instagram or WhatsApp link.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Drax (DRAX RAW), a filmmaker, video editor and photographer in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. Success: a visitor leaves convinced "this is the right person to hire" and messages Drax on WhatsApp.

## Positioning

Drax is the person, not a studio: one filmmaker who shoots, edits and colors every job. His proof is real work with real Tanzanian communities (school library and social impact films, event films, short-form reels), shot on a Sony A7C II.

## Product Principles

1. Drax first: the person behind the camera is the product.
2. Show the work, don't describe it: films play, photos fill the screen.
3. Phone first: every section must feel designed for a phone, not squeezed onto one.
4. One clear action: message Drax on WhatsApp.
5. Truth only: no invented clients, numbers, or specs.
